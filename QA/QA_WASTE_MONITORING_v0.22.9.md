# Waste monitoring — v0.22.9
- Dedicated waste measurement form.
- Waste category comes from central Waste Types library.
- Fields: date, department, category, weight kg, containers, patient days, responsible, document number, collection company, notes.
- Indicator calculated automatically as kg / patient days × 1,000.
- Patient-day period can be suggested from the central patient-day library.
- Registry shows category, weight, containers, indicator and document number.
- CSV export includes full traceability fields.
- Record drill-down shows measurement, indicator and collection/document data.
