# v0.25.6 — Greek IPC committee governance

Design base: v0.25.4 clean Committee workspace.

Managed structures:
- ΕΝΛ — Επιτροπή Νοσοκομειακών Λοιμώξεων
- ΟΕΕ — Ομάδα Εργασίας & Επέμβασης ΕΝΛ
- ΟΕΚΟΧΑ — Ομάδα Επιτήρησης Κατανάλωσης & Ορθής Χρήσης Αντιβιοτικών
- Δίκτυο Κλινικών Συνδέσμων
- Ομάδα Εκπαίδευσης IPC
- Custom committee/team

Governance:
- managed role, duties, institutional source, relationship and expected functions
- hospital selects actual employees and defines committee title + actual responsibility
- regular/alternate membership
- optional internal electronic approval from employee profile
- membership is ended, never physically deleted; reason/actor/time retained
- dedicated Role & Framework tab
- official template is separate from local composition/history

Important: the current catalog is a controlled application template based on the Greek national IPC framework. It is not a live government feed. Future official-source synchronization must use versioned source records and review/acceptance rather than silently changing existing committees.
