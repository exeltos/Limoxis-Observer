# v0.26.16 — Committees meeting workflow

- Preserves the normalized Limoxis design system from v0.26.15.
- Meeting creation includes regular/extraordinary classification.
- Meeting detail keeps structured attendance, quorum, topics, decisions, follow-up actions, minutes and approval workflow.
- Meeting list shows regular/extraordinary type.
- Finalization is blocked when the configured computable quorum is not met.
- Finalization still requires at least one present member and a decision/conclusion for every populated topic.
- Follow-up actions continue to be generated into Decisions & actions with owner/deadline/priority.
- Minutes approval remains bound to present committee members and the audit history is preserved.
