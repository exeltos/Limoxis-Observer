# Antiseptic Consumption — v0.23.3

- Dedicated antiseptic-consumption workflow.
- Product from central antiseptic library.
- Period, department, litres, documented data source/method.
- Patient-days from library or manual with source provenance.
- Automatic ABHR indicator: litres × 1,000 / patient-days.
- Non-ABHR products remain trackable but are excluded from ABHR indicator.
- Responsible person, reference/document number, notes and actor timestamps.
- Registry filters for period/product/source plus department/search.
- CSV export includes denominator, indicator and traceability.
- Structured record view for consumption, indicator and documentation.
- No fixed good/bad target hard-coded; future organization targets remain configurable.
- Prevention contextual return/highlight remains active.
